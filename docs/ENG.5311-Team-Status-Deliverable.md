#### Purpose and Delivery

Our team submits the completed *What Is Technical Communication?* frequently asked questions (FAQ) and its supporting documentation for review and potential future distribution. The project provides an accessible, one-page introduction to technical communication for individuals unfamiliar with the field. All final deliverables, research materials, design documentation, and revision records are organized in the team's GitHub repository.

We recommend transferring the project to Dr. Dayley, with Texas State University's Department of Technical Communication serving as its proposed long-term custodian.

#### Project Overview and Intended Audience

The project originated as an informational resource for prospective students considering Texas State University's Master of Arts in Technical Communication program. Audience research revealed an opportunity to develop a more broadly applicable resource that explains the profession to prospective students, academic advisors, faculty, employers, and other interested readers.

The final FAQ answers five foundational questions about technical communication, including its purpose, professional responsibilities, career opportunities, and everyday applications. A supplementary sidebar provides additional context, while the question-and-answer format allows readers to locate information quickly. The document supports both print and digital distribution.

#### Research, Usability, and Accessibility

The team conducted a Qualtrics survey that received **53 responses**, including **44 completed questionnaires**. Findings identified misconceptions about technical communication and informed the FAQ's emphasis on professional versatility, practical applications, and accessible explanations.

Five participants subsequently evaluated the FAQ's clarity and usability. All reported learning about technical communication and understanding the document. Their feedback supported retaining the question-and-answer structure and familiar examples while identifying improvements to the header image, footer readability, and editorial consistency.

Following the evaluation, the team rebuilt the document in Adobe InDesign to improve accessibility and implemented revisions involving text hierarchy, reading order, alternative text, image selection, and readability. Color-contrast testing exceeded **WCAG 2.1 AAA requirements** for normal text. Final screen-reader verification of the exported PDF remains unconfirmed in the project documentation and should occur before broader digital distribution.

#### Maintenance and Future Recommendations

The FAQ requires minimal routine maintenance, although future custodians should periodically review its content to ensure that professional roles, career descriptions, and examples remain accurate. Any substantial revisions should preserve the document's concise format, accessible language, and established visual hierarchy.

We recommend that future maintainers prioritize the following activities:

1. **Verify PDF accessibility:** Confirm screen-reader compatibility, document tagging, and reading order.
2. **Review content periodically:** Ensure accuracy and relevance of professional roles, career descriptions, and examples.
3. **Consider a Spanish-language version:** Expand the resource's accessibility and potential reach.
4. **Explore additional feedback mechanisms:** Consider a QR-code survey if the FAQ receives ongoing distribution.
5. **Develop a program-specific adaptation:** Create a version for MATC recruitment or advising if requested by the Department of Technical Communication.

Any revisions should be documented and retained with the existing project materials. Publication, distribution, and ongoing maintenance arrangements remain subject to departmental approval.

#### Transfer of Responsibility

With the completion of the Fall 2026 semester, our team has finalized the FAQ and assembled its supporting documentation for handoff. The GitHub repository provides a centralized record of the completed resource, its research foundation, design decisions, and revisions.

We recommend that Dr. Dayley review the final package and coordinate any subsequent distribution or maintenance with the Department of Technical Communication. No incoming student team has been assigned, and future development remains at the department's discretion.
