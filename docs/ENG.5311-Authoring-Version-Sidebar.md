## Authoring Version Sidebar

### Sidebar Title

#### An Overview of Technical Communication

### Sidebar Contents

Though technical communicators can take on many shapes, the overarching goal is to effectively communicate ideas and concepts to a specific audience.

The field was discovered during WWII when manuals were needed by the military to operate machinery. Since then, the need for technical communicators has grown into areas such as science, technology, government, healthcare, education, and finance.

Technical communicators work as translators, simplifiers, and connectors between complex information and people.
