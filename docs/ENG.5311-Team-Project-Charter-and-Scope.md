## Team Project Charter and Scope   

**Scope**   
Our semester project will be a **FAQ list** about Technical Communication as a Master’s degree, specifically for the Texas State University graduate program. We are considering multiple **audiences** between *non-specialist and experts*. Non-specialist would be potential graduate students who are interested in or need more information about the degree itself. Experts would be academic advisors and faculty members who have conversations with non-specialist concerning their academic/career goals. The **purpose** of the FAQ list, for both audiences, would be to provide an accurate and succinct tool that defines the degree, offers career insight/data, and highlight program outcomes (i.e. developed portfolio, exposure to field-specific software, etc.). The primary **constraint** impacting our proposed deliverable has been identified as *time management* since this group comprises combinations of students taking 2-3 courses, full time employees, and parents.
