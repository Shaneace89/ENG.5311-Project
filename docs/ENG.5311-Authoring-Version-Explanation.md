## Authoring Version Explanation

This document has been developed, organized, and styled with the intention of maximizing information, accessibility, and (re)usability across various audiences/users. The document itself is comprised of the following components: a title, the FAQ section, a sidebar (aligned to the right of the page, beside the FAQ section), and a footnote. This structure has been chosen from a mix of organizational priorities and to equitable separation of the three topics of information according to their use for the audience. Organizational decisions were made such as the traditional beginning with the title ("What is Technical Communication") that introduces the document's topic and form clearly, the middle of the document contains the primary content, and the footnote at the bottom of the page is a necessary, ethical component concerning the research methods utilized for this document's creation. Meanwhile, the spatial structure for the three sections of information have been divided accordingly: 

1. Primary Content (FAQ section): the largest section of the document.
   
2. Background Information (sidebar): aligned to the right of the primary content. It is smaller in width than the primary content, meant to signal that it is not the document's focus.

3. Research Methodology (footnote): This is the document's smallest section. It is not a section non-experts will need, however it is necessary for ethics and credibility to experts. 

Together these components can be (re)used across a diverse audience of experts and non-experts to communicate information about the field of technical communication. Maintenance for the document should be minimal but could be updated with new research, developments in the career field/job market, or if there was a need for this document to be targeted to a more specific audience (e.g. a small business conference, a college campus opening a technical communication program/certification, etc.).
